# Privacy Policy for NIMA NASHIK

Last updated: `2026-06-02`

NIMA NASHIK ("we", "our", or "us") provides a mobile application for association members, vendors, and administrators to access directories, events, circulars, banners, and moderated content workflows.

## Information We Collect

Depending on your role and how you use the app, we may collect:

- name
- email address
- phone number
- address
- company information
- vendor profile details
- member profile details
- uploaded content such as timeline posts, banners, brochures, images, and related metadata
- login and session information required for authentication and security

## How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- authenticate users
- manage role-based access
- display member and vendor directory information
- process timeline posts, app banners, and moderation workflows
- manage event, committee, and association administration
- improve operational communication inside the association

## Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be visible inside the app according to the user role, administrative approvals, and association workflows.

## Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures to protect stored data, including authenticated access controls and session-based account management.

## User Content

Members, vendors, and administrators may submit content such as posts, banners, brochures, images, and profile details. Submitted content may be reviewed, approved, rejected, or removed according to association moderation policies.

## Account Access and Changes

If you need to update account information, correct data, or request removal, please contact the association through the official support contact listed in the app or on the association website.
