### Short Description

### Full Description

NIMA NASHIK is the official association app for members, vendors, and administrators to stay connected with the latest updates, business activity, and association events.

With NIMA NASHIK, you can:

- explore association updates and committee information
- browse members and vendors
- view and manage events
- read circulars and announcements
- share approved timeline updates
- manage member, vendor, banner, and content workflows based on role

The app includes role-based experiences for:

- Members
- Vendors
- Association administrators

NIMA NASHIK helps the association keep communication, directory access, event coordination, and approvals organized in one place.
